How it works

Play together

Create one account for your team using a Lakeside email address. Everyone on the team may use the same team login.

Puzzles are grouped into six levels. Solve enough puzzles to unlock the next level. Answers ignore spaces, punctuation, and capitalization.

Each team may reveal one hint per calendar day and has five answer guesses for the entire hunt.
